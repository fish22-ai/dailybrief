Bond investors sit out ‘most consequential’ US midterm elections in years
FT Markets · 2026-11-01 13:00
查看英文摘要Show English excerpt
Big fund managers are pressing ‘pause’ as vote and economic data releases loom
发生了什么What happened
Large bond funds are sitting out what is being called the most consequential US midterm election in years, pressing pause as the vote and a run of economic data approach.
市场为什么在意Why it matters
Bond markets price the fiscal and inflation path. The result determines whether deficit expansion is feasible over the next two years, and waiting is itself a position — duration is cut and volumes shrink.
金融传导Transmission
Midterms approach with the outcome highly uncertain→large funds refuse to carry duration risk→portfolio duration is cut below benchmark→long-end bids thin out and liquidity dries up→term premium becomes hard to push down→funds rebuild positions once the result lands→yields swing sharply in a single session
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • "Sitting out" does not mean holding nothing; it means not making a directional bet. Funds keep a core position but run duration below benchmark and add it back after the event. That is why volume reads the mood better than holdings.
  • Why is event risk sharper for bonds? A bond's return is coupon plus capital gain, and a 20bp jump in yields can wipe out a year of coupon. Equity investors can wait; bond investors cannot.
  • The liquidity mechanism: market makers widen bid-ask spreads and cut inventory when uncertainty is high, so the same sell order moves price further. That is what "liquidity itself disappears" means.
  • Duration in plain terms: if rates rise 1%, a bond's price falls roughly duration%. For a ten-year duration, a 20bp rise in yields is about a 2% price decline. The longer the duration, the more sensitive the position to the election result.
  • A likely follow-up: where do bonds go afterwards? It depends on whether you get a split Congress (bond-friendly, deficit expansion constrained) or a one-sided result (bond-negative, fiscal and tariff risk both rise).

中期选举临近、结果高度不确定→大型基金不愿承担久期风险→组合久期被主动压低→长端买盘减少、市场流动性变薄→期限溢价易上难下→选举结果落地后集中建仓→收益率出现单日大幅波动
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • 「sit out」不是不持有，而是不做方向性押注：基金保留底仓，但把久期降到基准以下，等事件落地再补回来。所以看成交量比看持仓更能读出观望情绪。
  • 为什么事件风险对债市更明显？债券的收益结构是「票息 + 资本利得」，一次 20 个基点的收益率跳升，就可能吃掉一年的票息。股民可以等，债民等不起。
  • 流动性变薄的机制：做市商在不确定性高时扩大买卖价差、减少库存，于是同样的卖单会造成更大的价格冲击。这就是「流动性本身会消失」的含义。
  • 久期的近似含义：利率上升 1%，债券价格大约下跌 duration%。十年久期的债，收益率上行 20 个基点，价格约跌 2%。久期越长，对选举结果越敏感。
  • 面试可以接着答：选举后债市怎么走？关键看是「分裂政府」（利好债，赤字扩张受限）还是「一边倒」（利空债，财政与关税风险同时上升）。

Chart of the Week: Bond investors may cheer gridlock
FT Markets · 2026-10-31 18:30
查看英文摘要Show English excerpt
Midterms will have higher stakes for the bond market
发生了什么What happened
Investors are trimming growth bets and returning to bonds after a long sell-off, worried that the US economy is over-reliant on tech spending.
市场为什么在意Why it matters
This is the narrative flip from "AI is the growth engine" to "AI is concentration risk". Same fact, opposite direction for the risk premium — so allocations have to rebalance.
金融传导Transmission
US growth relies heavily on tech capex→investors worry about concentration risk→required equity risk premium rises→money leaves growth stocks→flows back into previously sold-off bonds→bond yields fall and prices recover→the discount rate falls, but earnings estimates are cut faster→the style rebalancing continues
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • What is concentration risk? When GDP growth or index returns depend heavily on a single sector, that sector's volatility becomes systemic risk, and diversification fails exactly when it is needed.
  • Why does money return to bonds? The stock-bond correlation is positive when inflation runs hot but turns negative when growth fear dominates — bonds become a hedge again. That, not "bonds got cheap", is the real driver.
  • Rebalancing is mechanical: when equities rally, they exceed target weight and the rules require selling equities and buying bonds. That amplifies style rotation independent of any fundamental view.
  • The formula:
    E(r) = r_f + ERP
    where ERP is the equity risk premium. When confidence in growth falls, the required ERP rises, so the same earnings justify a lower price.
  • A crisp point for interviews: this is not "bearish on AI", it is "unwilling to pay any price for AI". That is the difference between valuation and sentiment — one does the denominator, the other just tells a story.

市场为什么在意Why it matters

  • 什么是集中度风险？当 GDP 增长或指数涨幅高度依赖单一板块，这个板块的波动就变成了系统性风险，分散化在关键时刻失效。
  • 为什么资金回流债券？股债相关性在通胀高企时为正，但在增长担忧主导时会转负 —— 债券重新变回对冲工具。这是资金回流的根本原因，而不是「债券变便宜了」。
  • 再平衡的机械性：组合里股票涨太多会超过目标权重，规则要求卖出股票、买入债券。这会自动放大风格切换，与基本面判断无关。

Adjusting Imports of Robotics, Industrial Machinery, and Their Parts and Components Into the United States
Federal Register · 2026-11-02 00:00
查看英文摘要Show English excerpt
美国总统行政令 / 公告原文（Federal Register 一手文件）
发生了什么What happened
The US issued a presidential proclamation adjusting import measures on robotics, industrial machinery and their parts and components.
市场为什么在意Why it matters
Robots are a core input for reshoring manufacturing. Tariffing them raises the cost of US automation and contradicts the reindustrialisation goal — a tension inside the policy itself.
金融传导Transmission
US adjusts import measures on robotics and machinery→imported equipment costs rise→returns on automation investment fall→capex gets postponed→domestic capacity expansion slows→labour cost pressure builds→services prices stay sticky→core disinflation slows
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • Why does taxing capital goods backfire? The tariff protects domestic equipment makers, but the users are manufacturers. If there is no domestic substitute capacity, the result is pure cost inflation rather than import substitution.
  • Robot density is industrial robots per 10,000 manufacturing workers, the standard gauge of automation. South Korea and Singapore lead; the US trails Japan and Germany.
  • The capex decision rule:
    NPV = ∑ CFₜ/(1+r)ᵗ − initial outlay
    Higher equipment prices raise the outlay directly, pushing marginally positive projects negative, so they get deferred.
  • The inflation lag: import prices reach PPI within one to three months and CPI typically three to six months later. The effect on core CPI is therefore gradual and easily underestimated early on.
  • A closing thought: industrial policy's goals and instruments often conflict — protecting domestic manufacturing while taxing the equipment that manufacturing needs.

FBI blames North Korea for stolen billions from Binance in biggest hack in history
SCMP · 2026-11-01 07:49
查看英文摘要Show English excerpt
The theft of billions of dollars from Binance within a matter of minutes, the biggest cryptocurrency heist in history, was orchestrated by North Korea
发生了什么What happened
The FBI attributed the theft of billions of dollars from Binance — the largest crypto heist in history — to North Korean hackers, with funds moved within minutes.
市场为什么在意Why it matters
This is no longer a technical vulnerability story; it is a state actor using crypto as a funding channel. What it raises is compliance cost across the industry and the risk premium on exchanges.
金融传导Transmission
A state-backed hacking group attacks an exchange→hot-wallet keys or signing flows are compromised→assets are moved across chains within minutes→the exchange halts withdrawals and covers the shortfall→regulators demand stricter custody and proof of reserves→compliance and insurance costs rise→smaller exchanges are squeezed out→industry concentration increases
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • Why is "minutes" the key word? On-chain transfers are irreversible, and once funds enter a mixer or get split across many addresses, recovery odds collapse. The control window is minutes, and after-the-fact remedies are of limited use.
  • Proof of Reserves shows assets covering deposits, typically via a Merkle tree. It cannot prove liabilities, nor that the assets have not been rehypothecated — that is its fundamental limit.
  • Why does North Korea steal crypto? It bypasses SWIFT and dollar clearing, and once converted the proceeds fund procurement and financing. Under sanctions it is the cheapest alternative financial channel.
  • The hot-versus-cold wallet trade-off: hot wallets are online and convenient for withdrawals but expose an attack surface; cold wallets are offline and safe but illiquid. The balance point is really user experience versus security.
  • A point worth making: the market impact of such events is not the coin price but the trust premium — capital concentrates in compliant platforms with transparent reserves, and smaller venues get squeezed out systematically.

Supersonic airliner in doubt as company shifts to powering AI data centers
Ars Technica · 2026-10-31 05:41
查看英文摘要Show English excerpt
Boom’s airliner may be delayed, and its data center power business lost a customer.
发生了什么What happened
Supersonic airliner developer Boom's aircraft programme may slip as the company pivots to powering AI data centres — a business that has just lost a customer.
市场为什么在意Why it matters
Expected returns in AI infrastructure are now high enough to make an aviation company switch to selling power. That is a textbook misallocation signal and among the hardest evidence in the bubble debate.
金融传导Transmission
AI data-centre power demand surges→power supply becomes the bottleneck→power premiums rise and payback periods shorten→capital shifts from long-cycle R&D to shorter-payback businesses→the supersonic programme loses resources→the project slips and engineering talent leaves→long-term innovation capacity is squeezed by near-term cash flow
展开解析Show analysis收起解析Hide analysis5 条5 notes
  • This is capital migrating to the bottleneck: when one link is in shortage, the highest return on capital sits there — even if it has nothing to do with the company's existing technical capability.
  • Why is power the AI bottleneck? A single AI data centre can draw hundreds of megawatts, the load of a small city. In some US states, grid interconnection queues now run for years.
  • Sunk cost versus the pivot decision: R&D already spent on the aircraft is sunk, and a rational decision looks only at future marginal cash flow. In practice management stays anchored to sunk costs until cash flow forces the turn.
  • The test for a bubble is not valuation level but whether capital flows into areas where the company has no comparative advantage. A supersonic aircraft maker selling data-centre power is exactly that signal.
  • A closing line: the AI bottleneck is migrating from chips to power, then the grid, then land and permits — and the further along you go, the more capital-intensive, lower-margin and more regulated the business becomes.
